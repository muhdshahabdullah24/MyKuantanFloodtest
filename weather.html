Useful context

Weather and flood-risk alerts

Weather conditions are one factor in the flood-risk system. Automatic alerts require both a saved area overlapping Category 4 or Category 5 flood-risk polygons and a dangerous weather code.

  • Weather updates every five minutes while flood alerts are enabled.
  • Dangerous codes include heavy rain, violent rain showers, and thunderstorms.
  • Forecast information does not predict that a flood will occur.
